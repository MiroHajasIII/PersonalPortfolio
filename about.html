About me.

I'm Miro. A Software Engineering graduate with a 2:1 who loves getting into the gritty details of software development and web design when I can. A nice little 'proof is in the pudding' example of this could probably be when I finished my final year project for Uni. I managed to focus so much on trying to make an 'instant' browser-based code refactoring tool, to the point it was actually instant. I managed to make the whole refactoring part of this code in JavaScript, and it worked really well.

Right now I'm looking for a step into the professional software development realm, whether that's through actual software development, or web development. My options are open. The industry itself is the main goal, and I'm excited I get to even chase it.

Thinking back to when this all started, I was a young boy who managed to figure there's a certain logic, a pattern behind the games I was playing and that this must have meant something more. I grew up on a small farm in Slovakia, and moved to the UK when I was 5. I had my first console around the age of 7, and this is where the habit just grew. Fast forward to me trying simple coding in Java, and the road simply flew from there.

There was a distinct moment in my college Games Development module where I felt myself coming full circle to my childhood. We had a challenge from my teacher to make a 2-player 2D fighting platformer like similar stickmen games you might have seen before. As it turns out, I was the only one who could, and the feeling was amazing. It was then that I knew this was the thing for me.

One of the main things I enjoy about coding is the the journey from a Miro board (no pun intended) to a finished project. Not just the destination, but the journey itself. It's a combination of an idea yet to be done, and the dance of filling out pages full of content to use, to spending maybe too much time on the details like the animations, and other things that you could take hours perfecting for only a few people to notice.
